About Us

Welcome to Oru Cup Story, where every sip tells a story! Born from the love for authentic tea and homemade goodness, our little tea shop brings together the perfect blend of flavor, comfort, and happiness.

At Oru Cup Story, you’ll find more than just tea — we serve freshly baked cakes, crispy cookies, and tasty snacks made with love and the finest ingredients. Whether you’re here for a hot chai break, a sweet treat, or a quick snack with friends, we make sure every moment feels special.

Our mission is simple — to make every customer smile with the warmth of a perfect cup. Sit back, relax, and enjoy the cozy vibes, delicious aromas, and cheerful conversations that fill our shop every day.
